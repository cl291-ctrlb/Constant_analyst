🏒 NHL Team Performance Analysis (1990–1991)
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on scraping, cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing NHL team performance data for the 1990 and 1991 seasons. The goal is to extract meaningful insights related to team dominance, offensive strength, defensive performance, and overall balance.

🔧 Tools & Technologies

Python

BeautifulSoup (Web Scraping)

Pandas (Data Cleaning & Analysis)

Matplotlib & Seaborn (Visualization)

📊 Key Analysis & Insights

Dataset contains 25 teams per season

Chicago Blackhawks dominated in both 1990 and 1991

Pittsburgh Penguins scored the most goals in 1990

Best defensive teams:

1990: Quebec Nordiques

1991: Calgary Flames

Quebec Nordiques conceded 125 more goals than they scored in 1990

Chicago Blackhawks recorded a +25 goal difference in 1991

Balanced offense and defense leads to greater success

Strong positive correlation observed between goal difference and wins
